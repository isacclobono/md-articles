# Passing Microsoft Fabric DP-700 & DP-600 in 2026: 4 Production Architecture Traps Most Engineers Miss

## Introduction
You aced the labs. You know `spark.conf.set` versus `SET spark.sql.adaptive.enabled`. Yet the DP‑600/DP‑700 case studies rarely run on F2 SKUs with pristine data. The exams test *features*; production demands *constraints*. The gap between “it works in my workspace” and “it survives a Black Friday load on F64 capacity” is where architects earn their keep.

## Why This Matters
When a model fails under real traffic, the cost isn’t just a failed query—it’s lost revenue, broken SLAs, and a damaged reputation. Understanding the hidden constraints that govern scaling, security, and governance prevents the kind of outage that makes headlines.
